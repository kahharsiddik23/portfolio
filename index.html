01. Identity

Behind The Hunt

I'm Kahhar Siddik, a cybersecurity enthusiast and bug bounty hunter focused on discovering vulnerabilities in modern web applications and digital systems.

My approach combines systematic reconnaissance, creative security testing, vulnerability analysis, and automation. I don't just run tools—I dive deep into business logic to understand how complex systems fail.

I continuously learn, experiment, document findings, and improve my methodology to become a better security researcher. Whether it's mapping a massive attack surface or chaining small bugs for critical impact, I am driven by the puzzle of breaking systems to make them stronger.
